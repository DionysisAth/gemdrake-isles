Merge, hatch and restore in Gemdrake Isles, a cozy merge puzzle game with dragons that earn for you, even while you're away!

A storm has left the floating islands in ruins. Merge gems, plants and dragon eggs to fill orders from friendly characters, earn coins, and bring each island back to life, one bridge, windmill and lantern at a time.

MERGE TO MAKE MAGIC
• Drag two matching items together to make something better. Merge five at once for a bonus!
• Tap generators like the Crystal Mine and Seed Basket for new items.
• Clear fog, cobwebs and rubble to open up your board.

HATCH & GROW DRAGONS
• Hatch Earth, Water, Fire, Crystal and legendary Shadow dragons.
• Merge dragons to grow them from babies into mighty Gemdrakes.
• Your dragons fly around your island and fill the dragon hoard with coins while you're away.

RESTORE 5 MAGICAL WORLDS
• Meadow Ruins, Ember Volcano, Coral Lagoon, Crystal Peaks and Shadow Sky, each with its own look, music, items and characters.
• Watch the island change as you rebuild towers, gardens, wells and shrines.

SOMETHING NEW EVERY DAY
• Daily tasks and a 7-day login calendar.
• Weekly festivals with their own board, a reward track and an exclusive legendary dragon.
• Achievements, a Dragon Book to complete, and leaderboards.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Relaxing and cozy, with no forced ads: watch a video only when you want a reward.
• The exact odds of every random reward are shown in the game.
• Plays offline. Back up your progress with a code or cloud save.

Gemdrake Isles is free to play. Optional in-app purchases are available, including the Dragon Club monthly subscription, which you can cancel anytime in Google Play.
