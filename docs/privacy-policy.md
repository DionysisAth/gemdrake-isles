# Gemdrake Isles: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 30 September 2026_

Gemdrake Isles ("the game") is made by an independent developer ("we"). This policy
explains what information the game uses and why. We don't run our own servers, don't ask
you to create an account, and don't sell personal information.

## Information stored on your device

Your game progress, settings and purchase records are saved on your device. If you use
**Backup & restore**, the backup code contains your game progress and nothing else.

## Google Play Games and Apple Game Center

If you sign in to Google Play Games (Android) or Game Center (iOS), the game uses them for
leaderboards, achievements and cloud save. Your player name, scores, achievements and saved
game are handled by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies:

- Google: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>
- Apple: <https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>

## Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows **optional** video ads that you choose to watch for a reward. Ads are
provided by Google AdMob, which may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address,
device information and how you interact with ads, to show, measure and improve ads and to
prevent fraud. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), the game asks
for your consent first using Google's consent tool, and you can change your choice at any
time in **Settings → Privacy options**. On iOS, the game asks for permission before any
tracking, and works the same whether or not you allow it.

How Google uses this information: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>

## In-app purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your
payment details. The game receives only a confirmation of what was bought, so it can give
you your items.

## Other network requests

- **Time check:** the game asks a public Google web address for the current time, so
  timers can't be cheated by changing the phone's clock. Like any web request, this shares
  your IP address with that server.
- **Game settings updates:** the game may download updated game settings (prices, rewards)
  from a public web address we control. No personal information is sent.

## Notifications

Reminders (energy full, daily gift and so on) are scheduled on your device. You can turn
them off in the game's settings or your phone's settings.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal
information from children.

## Your choices

- Change ad consent in **Settings → Privacy options**, and reset or limit your advertising
  ID in your phone's settings.
- Delete all game data by uninstalling the game (and deleting any cloud save from your
  Google Play Games or Game Center account).
