# Gemdrake Isles: Player's Guide

Everything in the game and how it works, from a player's point of view.

---

## The idea in one minute

You find five floating islands in ruins. You **merge** matching items on a board to make better
items, deliver them to the island's characters for **coins**, and spend the coins to **restore
the island** piece by piece. Along the way you **hatch dragons** from eggs. Dragons live on your
island and **earn coins for you, even while the game is closed**. Grow them, collect every kind in
the Dragon Book, and travel from island to island.

Nothing in the game punishes you. There is no losing, no failing and nothing you can miss forever.
Waiting only means more is ready when you come back.

---

## Getting started

- **Tutorial:** the first few minutes guide you step by step: tap a generator, merge two items,
  deliver an order, restore your first piece of the island and learn about dragons. You can skip
  it from Settings.
- **You start with** 10 coins, 5 gems and 100 energy.

## The screen

- **Top bar:** your **level** (the star, with a ring that fills as you earn XP), **energy**
  (with a timer to the next point), **coins** and **gems**. Tap energy to get more, and tap gems
  to open the **Gem Shop**. The **calendar** button opens **Daily**, and the **gear** opens
  **Settings**.
- **Bottom tabs:**
  - **Board:** where you merge.
  - **Island:** restore the island and meet your dragons.
  - **Festival:** the weekly event (opens at level 4).
  - **Book:** the Dragon Book and Achievements.
- **Red badges** on tabs and buttons show that something is ready to collect.

---

## The Board

A 7 × 9 grid. At the start, much of it is covered, and you uncover it as you play.

### Controls

| Do this | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Drag** an item onto an identical item | They **merge** into the next level |
| **Drag** an item to an empty cell | It moves there |
| **Drag** onto a different item | They swap places |
| **Tap** a generator | It makes a new item (uses 1 energy) |
| **Tap** an item | The info bar below the board says what it is and what it becomes |
| **Tap** an order that's ready | Deliver it and collect the reward |
| **Drag** an item into a storage slot | Store it off the board |

### Merging

- **2 → 1:** two identical items become one item of the next level.
- **Bonus merge, 5 → 3:** if the item you drop onto is part of a connected group of at least 5
  identical items (counting the one you drag), all 5 become **3** of the next level instead of 2.
  That's one extra item for free. Grouping items pays off!
- Bigger merges look and feel bigger:
  - Every merge sends sparkles flying from the merged items into the result.
  - Bigger results add rings of light, rays, a small screen shake, confetti and a cheer, from
    **"Nice!"** through **"Great!"**, **"Awesome!"** and **"Amazing!"** up to **"LEGENDARY!"**.
  - Bonus merges and top-level items count extra.
- The merge sound rises in pitch as items get higher level.
- An item at its top level can't merge further. Top items are great for orders, and some have a
  special use (see below).

### Covered cells

| Cover | What it's like | How to clear it |
|---|---|---|
| **Fog** (clouds) | Hides what's underneath | Merge something next to it once |
| **Cobweb** | The item under it is visible but stuck | Merge next to it, or merge a matching item onto it |
| **Rubble** | Hides what's underneath, sturdier | Merge next to it twice |

Cleared cells often hold free items, sometimes even eggs.

### Generators

Generators make the basic items of their chain. Each tap costs **1 energy** and puts a new item
in the nearest empty cell.

- A generator has a number of **charges** (the badge on it). When they run out, it
  **recharges**. You'll see a countdown.
- **Speed up** a recharging generator with gems (1 gem per 3 minutes left) or by watching a
  video (up to 5 a day).
- **Upgrade** a generator with coins for more charges and faster recharging (3 levels:
  20 / 24 / 28 charges, 10 / 9 / 8 minutes).
- Tap a generator and then the **(i)** to see exactly what it can drop and the odds.
- Generators mostly drop level-1 items, sometimes level 2. They can also drop:
  - **treasure coins** from level 2,
  - **dragon eggs** from level 3,
  - **energy potions** from level 3.

| Generator | Makes | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| **Crystal Mine** | Gems | Start of the game |
| **Seed Basket** | Plants | Level 2 |
| **Ember Forge** | Tools | Ember Volcano (island 2) |
| **Tide Pool** | Shells & Pearls | Coral Lagoon (island 3) |
| **Geode Cavern** | Geodes | Crystal Peaks (island 4) |
| **Star Well** | Starlight, and rarely **Legendary Eggs** | Shadow Sky (island 5) |

### Item chains

| Chain | Levels |
|---|---|
| **Gems** | Pebble → Shard → Crystal → Cut Gem → Jewel → Crown Jewel → Heart Gem |
| **Plants** | Seed → Sprout → Bush → Fruit Tree → Magic Tree |
| **Tools** | Nail → Hammer → Toolbox → Anvil → Workbench |
| **Shells & Pearls** | Sand Dollar → Seashell → Conch → Oyster → Pearl → Pearl Crown |
| **Geodes** | Rough Geode → Split Geode → Amethyst Heart → Crystal Orb → Prism Star |
| **Starlight** | Stardust → Little Star → Moon Charm → Comet → Galaxy Orb |
| **Dragon Eggs** | Dragon Egg → Glowing Egg → Cracking Egg → *merge two to hatch a dragon* |
| **Legendary Eggs** | Starlit Egg → Moonlit Egg → Eclipse Egg → *merge two to hatch a dragon* |
| **Treasure** | Copper Coin → Coin Stack → Coin Bag → Treasure Chest → *tap Open!* |
| **Energy Potions** | Energy Drop → Energy Vial → Energy Flask → Energy Elixir → *tap Drink* |

- Tap any item and then **(i)** to see its whole chain. Levels you haven't made yet show as
  "???" until you discover them.
- **Treasure Chest:** tap **Open!** for 3 random rewards: coins, gems, energy, eggs or a Cut Gem.
  The odds are shown in Settings → Reward odds.
- **Energy potions:** tap **Drink** to turn them into energy right away (+5, +12, +30 or +70), or
  merge them first for a bigger boost.
- **Sell** any item for a few coins from the info bar. Items worth nothing show **Remove**
  instead.

### Storage and the gift box

- Below the board are **storage slots**. Drag items there to keep them safe and free up the
  board. You start with 3. Buy up to 3 more with gems (10, 20, 35), and island perks add more.
  Generators can't be stored.
- Items in storage count for orders too.
- The **gift box** on the left holds rewards that arrived while the board was full. Tap it to
  place the next one when there's room.

## Orders

Characters show up at the top of the Board with requests like "1 Cut Gem + 2 Sprouts".

- When you have everything (on the board or in storage), the order lights up. **Tap it to
  deliver.**
- Rewards are **coins and XP**, and sometimes a bonus: a **dragon egg**, **gems** or
  **energy**.
- You have 2 orders at once at first, 3 from level 3 and 4 from level 5. A new order replaces
  each one you finish.
- Items that an order needs get a small tick on the board.
- The characters: **Pip** (Island Caretaker), **Sage Ember** (Dragon Scholar, from level 2),
  **Brann** the Forgemaster (Ember Volcano), **Marina** the Coral Queen (Coral Lagoon),
  **Quartz** the Gem Sage (Crystal Peaks) and **Umbra** the Night Warden (Shadow Sky). Each
  asks for their island's items.

## Levels and XP

- You earn **XP** from orders and from restoring the island.
- **Each level up** gives **+3 gems** and **fully refills your energy**. It can also unlock new
  things, like a new generator, new characters, bigger orders, Daily tasks (level 2) and
  Festivals (level 4).

## Energy

- Tapping generators costs **1 energy**. Energy refills by **1 every 2 minutes** up to your
  maximum (**100**, raised by island perks).
- It keeps refilling while the game is closed.
- **When you run out:**
  - Watch a video for **+30 energy** (5 a day).
  - Refill to full for **15 gems**.
  - Drink an energy potion.
  - Wait.
- Level ups, some orders, chests, daily rewards and the shop also give energy. Rewards can take
  you **above** your maximum.

## The Island

The Island tab shows the island you're restoring, with your dragons flying around it.

### Restoring

- Each island has **10 restoration tasks**, such as "Clear the Vines" or "Fix the Old Bridge".
  Each costs coins and needs a certain level. Some tasks need an earlier one done first.
- Completing a task **changes the island right away**: vines disappear, bridges, wells, gardens,
  towers, windmills, lanterns and a shrine appear, and flowers and trees come back to life.
- Many tasks give a lasting **perk**:
  - **More max energy**
  - **Dragons earn more** (+10–15%)
  - **An extra storage slot**
  - **Longer offline earnings** (the dragon hoard holds more hours)
- Perks from every island you've restored keep working forever.

### The five islands

| # | Island | Look | New there |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Meadow Ruins** | Green meadow, river, round trees | Gems, Plants, Eggs, Pip and Sage |
| 2 | **Ember Volcano** | Lava river, glowing embers | Tools and the Ember Forge, Brann |
| 3 | **Coral Lagoon** | Sandy beach, palm trees, bubbles | Shells & Pearls and the Tide Pool, Marina |
| 4 | **Crystal Peaks** | Ice, crystal spires, snow | Geodes and the Geode Cavern, Quartz |
| 5 | **Shadow Sky** | Night sky, glowing trees, stars | Starlight, Legendary Eggs, the Star Well, Umbra |

- When all 10 tasks are done, a **Travel** button takes you to the next island. You get its
  generator, new orders and a new look. **Each island has its own music.**
- Use the **arrows** on the island to look back at islands you've already restored.
- Each island changes which dragons are likely to hatch there. For example, Fire dragons are
  more common on the Ember Volcano.

## Dragons

### Hatching

- Merge Dragon Eggs up to a **Cracking Egg**, then merge **two Cracking Eggs** to hatch a
  **dragon**. A bonus merge of 5 Cracking Eggs hatches **3 dragons**!
- The hatch reveal gets bigger for rarer dragons: rays, a flash of colour and confetti.
  **Rare, Epic and Legendary** hatches get a **Share** button that makes a picture card of your
  new dragon.
- On Meadow Ruins, eggs hatch into Earth 45%, Water 35%, Fire 15% or Crystal 5%. Other islands
  shift these odds. Every island's odds are shown in Settings → Reward odds.
- **Legendary Eggs** (from the Star Well) usually hatch the Legendary **Shadow** dragon (70%),
  otherwise Crystal (20%) or Fire (10%).

### Kinds of dragons

| Dragon | Rarity | Base coins per minute |
|---|---|---|
| Earth | Common | 2 |
| Water | Common | 2 |
| Fire | Rare | 4 |
| Crystal | Epic | 8 |
| Shadow | Legendary | 16 |
| Blossom | Legendary, festival only | 16 |
| Lumen | Legendary, festival only | 16 |

### Growing dragons

- On the Island tab, open **Dragons** and **tap two matching dragons** (same kind, same stage),
  or drag one onto the other, to grow them into the next stage:
  **Baby → Young → Adult → Elder → Gemdrake**.
- Each stage earns much more: ×1, ×2.5, ×6, ×14 and ×32. **Adults and older also earn gems**
  over time.

### Your dragons on the island

- Most dragons fly lazy laps around the island. Some **nap** (with little floating z's) and some
  **hop around playing**.
- **Tap a dragon:** babies **chirp**, and grown dragons **roar**.

## The Dragon Hoard (idle earnings)

- Your dragons earn coins (and later gems) **all the time, even when the game is closed**. The
  earnings build up in the **hoard** on the Island tab. Tap it to collect.
- The hoard fills up after **8 hours** at first. Island perks and the Gem Shop's hoard upgrades
  raise that to 12 or 24 hours, plus perks.
- **Welcome back:** after you've been away, a popup shows what your dragons earned. **Collect**
  it, or watch a video to **collect double**.

## Daily

Open it with the **calendar** button. It also pops up by itself once a day.

- **Login gift:** a 7-day calendar of gifts: coins, an energy potion, gems, an egg, more coins,
  energy, and on day 7 gems plus a Treasure Chest. Your **streak** counts the days you play in a
  row. Missing a day starts it again at day 1.
- **Daily tasks** (from level 2): 3 tasks a day, such as "Merge 50 times", "Deliver 5 orders",
  "Hatch 1 dragon" or "Open 1 treasure chest". Each gives a reward when you claim it. Targets grow
  as you level up.
- **All-done bonus:** finish and claim all 3 for 5 gems and a Treasure Chest, plus **extra gems
  that grow with your streak**.
- A new day starts at midnight on your phone's local time.

## Gem Shop

Tap the **gem counter** to open it. Gems are **only earned by playing**: orders, level ups,
daily tasks, chests, achievements, the Dragon Book and grown dragons. There's nothing to buy with
real money.

| Item | Cost | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Merge Chest | 12 | 3 random rewards (items, eggs, energy potion, coins) |
| Grand Chest | 35 | 6 random rewards with better eggs and gems (can include a Legendary Egg) |
| Egg Pack | 25 | 3 dragon eggs of different sizes |
| Legendary Egg | 90 | A Legendary Egg (from island 3 on) |
| Coin Pouch | 10 | 500 coins |
| Energy Pack | 10 | +60 energy |
| Bigger Dragon Hoard | 40, then 120 | The hoard holds 12h, then 24h (plus perks) |

- Tap **(i)** on any chest or pack to see the **exact odds** before buying.
- Spending more than 9 gems always asks you to confirm first.
- Other places to spend gems: extra storage slots, speeding up generators, refilling energy and
  the Festival's premium track.

## Festivals (weekly events)

Open from **level 4**, in the **Festival** tab.

- A new festival runs **every week, Monday to Sunday**. They take turns: the **Blossom
  Festival** and the **Lantern Festival**.
- Each festival has its **own board** and its own generator (the Blossom Tree or the Lantern
  Stall). Switch between **Island board** and the festival at the top of the Board tab.
  Festival items stay on the festival board; storage is for the island board only.
- Tapping the festival generator uses your normal energy.
- **Points:**
  - Merging festival items earns points: +1, +2, +4 or +7 depending on the level made.
  - **Offer** a top-level festival item for **+20**.
  - A bar shows your progress to the next reward.
- **Reward track:** 12 rewards, from 25 up to 1,500 points.
  - The **free track** ends with the festival's **exclusive Legendary dragon**: Blossom or Lumen.
  - The **premium track** unlocks for **80 gems** and adds extra gems, eggs, chests, energy, a
    Legendary Egg and more.
- Tap a reward to claim it once you've reached it. **Anything you reached but forgot to claim is
  sent to you automatically** when the festival ends.
- **Weekly leaderboard:** compare festival points with players everywhere through Google Play
  Games or Game Center (see "Online" below).

---

## Dragon Book and Achievements (Book tab)

### Dragon Book

- Every dragon at every stage: 7 kinds × 5 stages. Ones you haven't discovered show as
  **silhouettes**. Tap any of them for a hint or a description.
- **Rewards:**
  - **Hatch one of each** basic kind (Earth, Water, Fire, Crystal, Shadow) for **40 gems**.
  - **Raise a kind through every stage** for 10 gems (Common), 20 (Rare), 35 (Epic) or 60
    (Legendary).

### Achievements

**16 goals**, each with gems to claim when reached:

- **Firsts and counts:** first hatch, 25 hatches, 100 and 1,000 merges, 50 and 500 orders,
  10 bonus merges, 20 chests.
- **Milestones:** levels 10 and 25, reaching Ember Volcano and Shadow Sky, a 7-day streak,
  raising a dragon to Gemdrake, finishing a festival track, and discovering 18 dragons.

## Online (free, optional)

- **Google Play Games (Android) and Game Center (iOS)** can add:
  - **leaderboards** (festival points this week, highest level, dragons discovered, total merges),
  - **achievements** on your game account,
  - **cloud save**: your game is saved to your account when you leave the app, and if another
    phone has a newer save it offers to load it.

  *These switch on once the game is registered with Google Play and the App Store. Until then
  the leaderboard card and sign-in are hidden, and achievements still work inside the game.*
- **Correct time:** the game quietly checks the real time online, so changing your phone's clock
  doesn't skip ahead. Turning the clock back never gives anything either.
- The game works fully offline.

## Reminders (notifications)

Optional, and asked for once around level 3. The game can remind you when:

- your **energy is full**,
- the **dragon hoard is full**,
- a **new day's gift** is ready (10:00 in the morning),
- the **festival ends soon**.

Turn them off in Settings → Reminders. They come from your phone itself; nothing is sent
anywhere.

## Sharing

- **Share your island:** the share button in the island's corner makes a picture of your island
  with your level and dragon count.
- **Share a rare dragon:** after a Rare, Epic or Legendary hatch.

## Settings

- **Sound** on/off, and **Music** and **Effects** volume. The phone's volume buttons control the
  game's sound. Other music you're playing keeps playing, and the game goes quiet in the
  background and during videos.
- **Vibration** on/off.
- **Reminders** on/off.
- **Google Play Games / Game Center:** sign in, leaderboards and achievements (when available).
- **Backup & restore:** copy your whole game as a **backup code**, or send it to yourself, and
  paste it on any phone to restore your game.
- **Privacy options** for ads, where required.
- **Reward odds:** the exact odds for eggs on your current island, Legendary Eggs and Treasure
  Chests. Generator odds are shown by tapping each generator.
- **Skip tutorial** and **Reset progress**. Reset asks you first.

## Rewarded videos

Always your choice, never forced:

- **Collect double** idle earnings (welcome back).
- **+30 free energy** (5 a day).
- **Finish a generator's recharge** now (5 a day).

*In this build they are Google test ads or a short placeholder; real ads are not set up.*

## Saving

Your game saves after every move and when you leave the app, so nothing is lost if you close it.
Progress lives on your phone; use a **backup code** or **cloud save** to keep it safe.

## Not yet implemented (from the original design document)

**Left out on purpose (payments and ads):**

- **Real-money purchases:**
  - gem packs,
  - energy bundles,
  - the **Starter Pack**,
  - **event packs**,
  - chests or egg packs sold for money,
  - **VIP subscription** (daily gems, bigger energy cap, longer offline earnings),
  - purchase receipt checking,
  - "any purchase removes forced ads".
- **Real ads:** the video ads use Google's test ads or a placeholder. Real ad accounts, ad
  mediation and a real reward for watching aren't set up. There are no interstitial (forced)
  ads, which matches the design doc.
- **Paid premium event pass:** the festival's premium track is unlocked with earned gems, not
  money.

**Would need a server of your own (not free platform services):**

- **Leaderboard events in groups of ~50 players** with rewards for your rank. There are real
  weekly leaderboards (Play Games / Game Center), but no grouped competition with prizes.
- **Friends and gifting:** sending energy or items to friends.
- **Visiting friends' islands.**
- **Invite a friend**, where both players get a reward.
- **Remote push notifications** sent by the game's team, such as "Event ends in 2 hours" sent
  from outside. Reminders are local notifications only.
- **Analytics dashboards** (retention, funnels, where players get stuck, what they buy). The game
  only keeps a simple log on the phone.
- **A live remote-config service** to tune the economy and run A/B tests. The game can load
  updated settings from a simple web address, but there's no control panel or testing tool.
- **Fully server-verified time.** The game corrects the clock using public internet time, but
  there's no game server checking it.

**Not built yet (could be added without payments or servers):**

- **Egg packs for a specific dragon kind:** the shop's Egg Pack gives random eggs.
- **Board expansion:** making the 7 × 9 board bigger.
- **Decorating the island freely**, and decorations as event-pass rewards. Restoration follows
  the fixed tasks.
- **Story quests** with their own story line. The story is told through the island tasks and the
  characters' lines.
- **Rewarded video for a free chest** (the other video rewards exist).
- **Short video clips** of big merges and hatches for sharing. Sharing makes pictures only.
- **Event pass on a 4–6 week season.** Festivals run weekly instead.
- **Cloud save and leaderboards switched on:** built in, but they need the game registered in
  Google Play Console (one-time $25 developer fee) and App Store Connect (Apple developer account,
  $99/year), then the IDs added to the game.
- **Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt:** needs to be set up in the AdMob account, together
  with real ads.
- **Final art and audio:** all art is drawn by the game's code and all sound is synthesized. A
  professional artist or asset pack could replace them.
- **Soft launch and metrics** (Day 1 / Day 7 / Day 30 retention, conversion). These happen after
  release.
